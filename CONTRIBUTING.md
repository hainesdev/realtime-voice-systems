# Contributing

Contributions that improve documentation clarity, diagrams, synthetic examples,
or technical accuracy are welcome.

Before opening a pull request:

- Keep examples synthetic and free of real caller, customer, or infrastructure
  data.
- Do not add credentials, endpoints, phone numbers, recordings, or raw call
  transcripts.
- Describe factual changes with evidence and avoid unsupported reliability or
  compliance claims.
- Preserve Daniel Haines as the author of this portfolio material unless a
  specific third-party attribution is required.
